JOB DESCRIPTION
OSM Developer / Architect
STL
Role Summary
Develop and implement Oracle Communications Order and Service Management (OSM) solutions, preferably in the
telecommunications domain, and translate high-level requirements into effective technical designs and deployments.
Experience Requirement
  Minimum 10 years of working experience with OSM 7.3.x and above; telecom-domain experience is preferred.
 
Key Responsibilities & Skills
  Bring 2–8 years of experience in the IT telecommunications industry, as stated in the source requirement.
  Demonstrate good knowledge of Communications and OSS domains.
  Implement solutions involving OSS products such as RODOD, RSDOD, and NRM.
  Work with implementation technologies including Java, XML, XQuery, web services, WebLogic, and RDBMS.
  Build code and deploy completed code into development and testing environments.
  Translate requirements in high-level designs (HLDs) into effective solutions.
  Prepare HLDs, low-level designs (LLDs), and other project documentation.
  Have hands-on experience with OSM installation and troubleshoot installation and configuration issues.
  Work in UNIX/Linux environments and use Unix shell scripts.
  Understand TMF/eTOM, SID Data Model, and Oracle RODOD/RSDOD solutions.
  Work independently and collaboratively with strong communication and interpersonal skills.
