JOB DESCRIPTION
SAP FI/CO Consultant
STL
Role Summary
Participate in SAP S/4HANA Finance implementation, enhancement, and support. Translate finance business
requirements into SAP solutions and serve as a link between finance users and technical teams.
Key Responsibilities
  Lead and participate in full-lifecycle SAP implementations, rollouts, and upgrades focused on SAP S/4HANA Finance.
  Configure and customize FI-GL, FI-AP, FI-AR, FI-AA, FI-BL, CO-CCA, CO-IO, and EC-PCA; CO-PC experience is a
 significant plus.
 Analyze business requirements and design and document target (“to-be”) business processes.
  Recommend scalable SAP solutions aligned with business needs and global standards.
  Manage integration points between FI/CO, MM, SD, PP, PM, and external systems.
  Resolve finance-related incidents, perform root-cause analysis, and implement corrective actions.
  Develop test scenarios, lead UAT, and prepare end-user documentation and training materials.
  Manage workstreams or small projects, meet delivery commitments, and mentor junior consultants.
 
Required Qualifications & Experience
  Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Business Administration, Information Technology, or a related field.
  5–8 years of SAP FI/CO consulting experience, including at least one end-to-end full-lifecycle SAP implementation.
