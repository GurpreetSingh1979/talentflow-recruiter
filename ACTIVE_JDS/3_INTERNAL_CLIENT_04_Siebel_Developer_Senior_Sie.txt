Siebel Developer / Senior Siebel Developer / Siebel

STL
Role Summary
Design, build, configure, integrate, and support Siebel CRM solutions. Own technical delivery, resolve complex issues,
and collaborate with business, QA, and integration teams.
Key Responsibilities
  Configure Siebel business components, business objects, applets, views, screens, user properties, and other
 repository objects.
 Develop and maintain Siebel workflows, business services, eScript, and validation logic.
  Design and troubleshoot inbound and outbound integrations using EAI, integration objects, data maps, SOAP/REST
 services, XML, and JSON.
 Plan and execute EIM data loads, IFB runs, updates, exports, and migration activities; investigate failed batches and
 reconcile results.
 Prepare technical designs and effort estimates, perform code reviews, and support deployments throughout the SDLC.
  Diagnose application, integration, and performance issues using Siebel logs and Oracle SQL/PLSQL.
  Mentor developers and coordinate with QA, business analysts, and external-system teams to resolve defects and
 deliver changes.

  10+ years of Siebel CRM development experience, with strong hands-on CSW, EAI, and EIM skills.
