JOB DESCRIPTION
ADP WorkForce Suite Integration Engineer
STL — Fresenius Medical Care
Role Summary
Design, configure, test, deploy, and support hands-on interfaces between ADP WorkForce Suite and Workday, ADP, and
other connected HR, payroll, scheduling, reporting, identity, and enterprise platforms.
Experience Requirements
  Approximately 10 years of relevant integration, HR technology, payroll technology, or enterprise application
 experience.
 Demonstrable hands-on experience creating or supporting integrations within ADP WorkForce Suite / WorkForce
 Software Time.
 Experience with time-and-attendance inputs and outputs, including employee, organizational, time-entry, schedule,
 balance, activity, and payroll-related data.
 Experience with integration design, field mapping, transformation rules, reconciliation, exception handling, and
 production monitoring.
 Experience supporting integrations through testing, deployment, hypercare, and production stabilization.
  Programming experience using JavaScript.
  Experience with SQL and complex queries.
 
Mandatory Training & Product Credentials
  Successful completion or valid certification in Interfaces Fundamentals (INTERFACES101); evidence must be provided
 before profile submission.
 Interfaces Configuration (INTERFACES201) is strongly preferred.
  INTERFACES101 covers standard time-and-attendance interfaces and basic imports/exports, including HR,
 bank-balance, activity-tracking, time-entry
